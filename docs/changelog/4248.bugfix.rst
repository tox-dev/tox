A direct URL entry in :ref:`requires` (such as ``tox @ file:///path/to/tox.whl``) now triggers provisioning unless the
installed distribution's ``direct_url.json`` records the requested source, including any archive hash, subdirectory, or
VCS revision. When the installer omits credentials from the origin record, a credentialed direct URL without a requested
archive hash now fails with an explanation instead of hanging during provisioning.
